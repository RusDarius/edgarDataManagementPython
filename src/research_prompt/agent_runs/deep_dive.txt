DEEP DIVE (agent) — {IDS}
Question: {QUESTION}
Folder: {DIR}
Skill: .claude/skills/deep-dive-agent/SKILL.md (read it first; it is the contract for this run).

The data step already ran (generic_utils.name_dossier): {DIR}/dossier.md holds every measured fact the project has on these
names, and {DIR}/dossier.judgment.skeleton.json is the judgment shape with the data levels pre-filled. Your job is the part
the tools cannot do: the outside context, the catalysts and the trajectory call, proved against the data.

1. Read {DIR}/dossier.md once. Do not read the CSVs or dossier.duckdb whole; query one table with
   `python src/generic_utils/tv_scan_cli.py named --db {DIR}/dossier.duckdb ...` only when a number is missing from the md.
2. Outside read (WebSearch, standard first; extended only for thin, niche or very recent items). Run the research plan at the
   end of the md for each name, plus the QUESTION queries when a question is set. Collect: dated catalysts (earnings date and
   what the quarter must show, guidance, investor days, product / contract dates, regulatory and legal dates, index changes,
   lockups, dividends and buybacks), estimate and target changes, competitive and pricing news, management / capital
   allocation, macro or policy items that hit the industry. Every item is OUTSIDE with its link. Check each one against the
   dossier before you lean on it (a "beat" the prints table does not show, an upgrade the street fields do not show, a
   demand story the revisions contradict: say so).
3. Per name, write the judgment (start from the skeleton, save as {DIR}/dossier.judgment.json):
   - thesis (3-5 sentences: what the price assumes, what the data says, what outside could change it), bull and bear
     (each line cites a dossier number or an OUTSIDE source);
   - classes: SHORT (1-2 weeks), MID (1-3 months), LONG (6-12+ months), each DATA_BACKED / MIXED / NARRATIVE.
     DATA_BACKED = that horizon's measured arrows lean one way (up and down counts differ by 2+) AND the outside read agrees;
     NARRATIVE = most of that horizon's items are n/a or the call rests on outside items alone; else MIXED;
   - trajectory: one row per horizon with path (words), price_lo / price_mid / price_hi and evidence. Anchor the prices on
     the dossier: SHORT on the analog 7d / 14d px_q10 / q50 / q90 and the alpha 7d band; MID on the analog 28d / 56d paths,
     the alpha 28d band and the street target; LONG on forward value bear / base / bull and the cycle drop. Move off an anchor
     only with a stated reason (an OUTSIDE item or a measured conflict). When the all-floor and same-industry analogs
     disagree, say which you follow and why;
   - levels: entry_low / entry_high (entry book or bounce band; prefer the larger margin of safety), kill (on a close;
     kill_px or bounce_kill, else below the analog 28d q10 price, stated), target_short / target_mid / target_long;
   - verdict (BUY_BAND / ACCUMULATE / WAIT / AVOID / TRIM / SHORT_WATCH), grade (High / Med / Low), horizon (the one you
     act on), plan (what to do at which price and date, what invalidates it), size (starter about $5,000; an add about
     $1,000; a staged tranche plan is fine);
   - catalysts: dated rows with direction (up / down / either) and source; outside: the OUTSIDE lines with sources.
4. Suite: `order.ids` puts the names in the order you would act on them and `order.by` says the one key that sets it
   (for example: up to target_mid over down to the kill, both from the levels) — a stated order, never a blended score.
   `summary` = the read in 3-6 sentences (title, text, tone). With a QUESTION, `question_read.text` answers it and
   `question_read.rows` (headers Item / Read / Source) lays out how it plays: timeline, mechanics, valuation anchors,
   which listed names carry the exposure and how (stakes, suppliers, competitors), flows (index, lockup), the trade at each
   stage (before, day one, after the lockup), what would make it fail.
5. Rules: stock ids are EXCHANGE:TICKER everywhere. No 0-100 score; arrows are counts. A deep dive never opens a second
   budget: a BUY_BAND / ACCUMULATE is a candidate for the Daily's queue (0-2 NEW), with its band and kill. AI / data-center
   names stay in their own lane (contagion, late 2026). Give the most critical read: the gain to take, the loss to let go.
   If a dated OUTSIDE event touches a held line, add it to config/generic_utils/catalyst_calendar.json (date, kind, event,
   symbols or exposure, source, read) so event-risk sees it.
6. Render and fix: `python src/generic_utils/tv_scan_cli.py dossier-page --dir {DIR} --strict` (0 warnings). Chat: a short
   snapshot per name (verdict, band, kill, the horizon you act on and its class, the one catalyst that matters) and the
   path of the HTML.
