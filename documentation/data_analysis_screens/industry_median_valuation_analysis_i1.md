## Reasoning & Methodology Notes

### Why Focus on Median (Not Mean)?

1. **Median is Robust:** Single outlier cannot shift median; mean can be distorted by 1-2 mega-cap players
2. **Cohort Quality:** Median reflects the "typical" company in an industry (important for replicable strategy)
3. **Mean-Median Gap = Red Flag:** When mean >> median, indicates hidden structure (need to drill into sub-segments)

### Why Size Matters (Sample n)

- **n < 10:** Unreliable across-the-board (single company can dominate)
- **n = 10-30:** Acceptable for specialized sectors; but validate sector structure
- **n > 30:** Robust; minor outlier risk; more confidence in median stability

### Why Mean-Median Alignment Matters

| Mean-Median Gap | Signal |
|-----------------|--------|
| < 10% | **Excellent cohesion**—all companies relatively similar valuation |
| 10-30% | **Good**—normal distribution; some outliers but not concerning |
| 30-50% | **Caution**—suggests sub-segmentation or clustering |
| > 50% | **Major red flag**—likely bipartite structure; manual drilling required |
