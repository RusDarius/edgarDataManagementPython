---------------------------------------

  weeks_progression/     weeks_path, weeks_span, weeks_breadth

Compile only if inspect sources differ. Do not re-fish DuckDB.
Peers and street/model come from the latest wisdom value-tech and forward

Mesh is letters in order F C P D T, only the ones that are on. `-` means
none. nmesh is the count of letters. Mesh 3+ is nmesh >= 3. It is not a score.
Copy the on/off rules from the canvas Ledger skill. Do not invent a second rule.

Best 25 is judgment on the base 100. Cite mesh, dte, 25-week bo change
(bo_last - bo_first), both valuation reads, and leftover.
Cite street_left beside left. `generic_utils.derive.leftover_carrier`
is `edge_only` when street_left is negative and left is positive.
Do not put an edge_only name in the Best 25.
Trailing TV, taken as published: pe_peer with pe_vs_ind, evebitda_peer with evebitda_vs_ind.
Forward derivative, which can be off: pe_fwd_peer with pe_fwd_vs_ind, evebitda_fwd_peer with evebitda_fwd_vs_ind.
Setup peer with val_vs_ind stays the one quoted multiple. Mesh D uses that peer only.
DISCOUNT uses that column's median percent only (<= -5). val_vs_avg is
the gap versus the industry average and stays its own column. ABOVE/BELOW
is cited with vs50.
The Ledger names each *_peer column.
A mesh of 5 with paid range is
still a watch. Mix avoid_value_trap on a live leftover Book line stays
HOLD_NO_ADD. Shorts stay on the short sleeve. Do not short a leftover-rich name.
